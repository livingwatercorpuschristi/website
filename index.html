How an atmospheric water generator works

An atmospheric water generator (AWG) makes water from the humidity in the air, the same way condensation forms on a cold glass.

Output depends on humidity and temperature. It's highest when the air is warm and humid and lower when the air is cool or dry. The unit runs on electricity.
